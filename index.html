Featured project

Shivaliva

Godot 4.7 · Windows · public alpha v2.0.5 on itch.io · Steam page coming soon · solo developer and director · site and press kit: play.shivalivaleveling.com

A space-faring social life sim in the spirit of Yohoho! Puzzle Pirates. Islanders keep their own routines, twelve mini-games pay out in coin, and the coin buys a house, clothes and a passage between the islands.

  • Live conversationIslanders answer free-text chat through a live LLM and remember you. Their voices are generated on the player's own machine. Affinity unlocks new interactions, up to hugs and holding hands.
  • Jobs and mini-gamesDish duty, mining, sailing, gunnery, carpentry, navigation, sparring and Texas Hold'em against AI opponents, all on one coin economy.
  • Build and dressBuy a lot, raise a house and furnish it. A wardrobe with dyes.
  • The voyageSail between islands through the Stardust, with duties on board and raider battles.
  • The labyrinthPuzzle-battle bouts against its beasts, ending in a boss fight.

How it's built

Solo, with an AI-assisted workflow: I set the design and the quality bar, direct the code with Claude Code, and test every feature in the game. The core games are covered by automated test scenes and headless simulations. The playing-card deck ships with a checker that measures the gap between every element on all 52 faces.
